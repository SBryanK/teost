Session 1 · Personal brand & LinkedIn

LinkedIn harus membuat recruiter cepat memahami: siapa kamu, apa expertise-mu, dan bukti apa yang kamu punya.

Define Personal Brand

Personal brand bukan slogan. Bangun dari Skills / Expertise + Activities / Evidence.

  • Skills: domain, tools, technical expertise, transferable skills.
  • Activity: projects, school, community, competitions, volunteering, achievements.
  • Consistency: headline, About, Experience, Featured, posts, dan Recommendations harus menceritakan arah yang sama.

Banner & Professional Photo

  • Banner harus align dengan professional identity; hindari foto graduation/liburan jika tidak mendukung positioning.
  • Keep it clean. Satu message lebih kuat daripada banner penuh teks.
  • Canva/AI design tools boleh dipakai untuk menghasilkan visual yang profesional.
  • Foto: wajah jelas, lighting baik, natural smile, personality tetap terlihat.
  • remove.bg berguna bila hasil cut-out tetap natural.

Connect vs Follow

Connect adalah hubungan dua arah; setelah diterima kalian menjadi 1st-degree connections. Follow satu arah untuk melihat public posts tanpa menjadi direct connection.

Untuk alumni, coworker, event contact, atau orang yang sudah pernah ngobrol: connect biasanya lebih bernilai. Untuk thought leader yang belum kenal: follow dulu, engage secara relevan, baru connect bila ada context.

Experience & Recommendations

Experience jangan sekadar copy job description. Gunakan struktur:

Action + Scope + Method + Result

Recommendations adalah social proof. Minta dari orang yang benar-benar pernah melihat cara kamu bekerja: manager, project lead, colleague, stakeholder, mentor.

LinkedIn Headline

Headline sebaiknya mengandung semantic structure yang jelas untuk recruiter search dan manusia:

Target identity → Core expertise → Differentiator/evidence → Optional personality word

Example: Solutions Architect | Cloud Security & Edge Platforms | Enterprise Customer Delivery | Curious

Gunakan power words dan achievement bila relevan, tetapi jangan keyword-stuffing. Kejelasan positioning lebih penting.

Grow beyond 1st-degree connections

2nd-degree connections sangat bernilai untuk warm introductions, alumni outreach, dan shared-interest networking. Jangan mengoleksi connection secara random; bangun jaringan orang yang relevan dengan target role dan industry.

Content tip: pilih 2–3 content pillars dan share/post topik yang konsisten supaya brand terlihat coherent.

Career fit

VIPS Framework

V — Values

Apa yang paling kamu pedulikan dan memberi sense of purpose?

I — Interests

Topik/aktivitas yang secara natural menarik perhatianmu.

P — Personality

Bagaimana kamu bekerja paling baik: structure, ambiguity, teamwork, risk.

S — Skills

Hard technical capabilities dan soft transferable skills yang terbukti.

Career transition check: pertimbangkan Wealth, Health, Ability, dan Time. Pivot yang bagus harus feasible, bukan hanya menarik.

Session 2 · Communication & networking

“You are as good as you can communicate” — tetapi komunikasi bukan sekadar banyak bicara.

Audience

Siapa mereka? Agenda, concern, expertise, dan apa yang paling penting bagi mereka?

Message

Punch line, content, flow, depth, visual.

Delivery

Script, voice, body language, pacing, anticipated questions.

Content & Channel

Clarity · Correctness · Concision.

Pilih channel berdasarkan urgency, sensitivity, need for record, dan complexity: email, WhatsApp/chat, 1:1, atau group.

Email rule: dalam dua baris pertama, pembaca sudah harus mengerti inti dan action yang dibutuhkan.

DISC as a communication heuristic

  • D — Dominance: short, concise, results + recommendation.
  • I — Influence: conversational, expressive, energetic.
  • S — Steadiness: build trust, give continuity/reassurance.
  • C — Conscientiousness: detailed, logical, evidence-driven.

Adapt, don’t impersonate. Sedikit effort menyesuaikan communication style sudah cukup; tetap jadi diri sendiri.

7% words · 38% vocal · 55% non-verbal — jangan digeneralisasi

Angka Mehrabian sering disalahartikan sebagai “hanya 7% komunikasi berasal dari kata”. Itu tidak berlaku untuk semua komunikasi. Temuan tersebut terkait komunikasi feelings/attitudes, terutama saat verbal dan non-verbal tidak congruent.

Untuk technical explanation, legal instruction, angka, coding, dan spesifikasi, wording jelas tetap sangat penting. Lesson yang tepat: pada komunikasi interpersonal sensitif, words, tone, dan body language sebaiknya konsisten.

Workplace Networking

↑ Up

Manager / senior leader

↔ Lateral

Cross-functional peers

↓ Frontline

Technicians, operators, admin

↗ External

Supplier, customer, ecosystem

Jangan hanya network “ke atas”. Operational people sering memiliki informasi paling nyata tentang bagaimana proses benar-benar berjalan.

Trust before favour

  1. Temukan common ground.
  2. Engage dengan genuine question.
  3. Follow up / contribute value.
  4. Baru minta advice, introduction, atau referral.

Catatan “3 outreach lalu ke-4 minta favour” lebih baik dipahami sebagai prinsip earned trust, bukan aturan mekanis.

Job search operating system

Jangan apply dan berharap. Kelola pipeline seperti funnel.

LinkedIn job-search loop

  1. Join groups/communities relevan.
  2. Follow target companies.
  3. Set job alerts and notifications.
  4. Apply deliberately; tailor evidence ke role.
  5. Track conversion dan belajar dari data.

Track every application

Simpan: industry, company, job title, application date, portal/referral, job description, stage, contact, follow-up date, outcome.

Interview rate = Interviews ÷ Applications

Workshop memberi rule of thumb sekitar 1 interview per 15 applications. Gunakan sebagai rough diagnostic, bukan universal benchmark; conversion bergantung industry, seniority, geography, eligibility, dan kualitas application.

CAR for resume · STAR for interview

CAR

Context: situation/challenge.

Action: langkah spesifik dan skill yang kamu gunakan.

Result: measurable outcome/value.

STAR

Situation → Task → Action → Result.

Action dan Result biasanya harus paling kuat. Hindari menjawab dengan “we” terus-menerus; interviewer perlu tahu kontribusi personalmu.

Cover Letter

Workshop guideline: strict 1 page, maksimal 5 paragraphs.

Why role → Your fit → Evidence → Why company → Close

Closing cukup sederhana: thank them for their time, reaffirm interest, dan state bahwa kamu look forward to discussing the experience further during an interview.

Pitfall: claim tanpa evidence. “I am a strong leader” jauh lebih lemah daripada contoh nyata saat memimpin ambiguity, conflict, atau delivery.

Session 3 · Interview

Organise yourself, research deeply, answer with structure.

Important details before interview

  • Time, timezone, format, exact location.
  • Name dan position interviewer.
  • Duration dan likely stages.
  • Documents/portfolio yang harus dibawa.
  • Assessment type: behavioural, technical, case, test, panel.
  • Dress code.
  • Company business model, products, customers, competitors, recent priorities.
  • Role outcomes, requirements, likely pain points.

Three answer frameworks

Present → Past → Future: untuk “Tell me about yourself”.

3 Points Technique: pilih tiga poin utama supaya jawaban tidak berputar-putar.

STAR: untuk behavioural questions.

Good questions for Talent Acquisition

  • What distinguishes graduates who perform strongly in their first 6–12 months here?
  • At this stage, what capabilities are you assessing most closely?
  • How is the team structured, and who would this role work with most frequently?
  • What learning curve tends to be hardest for new joiners?
  • What does a strong first 90 days look like?
  • Based on our conversation, is there an area of my profile you would like me to elaborate on?
Better than asking “What is my differentiator from other candidates?” Recruiters usually cannot openly compare candidates. Ask instead what differentiates high performers, then show your evidence.

30-day execution plan

Reading is not execution.

Week 1 — Position

Isi VIPS, tentukan 2–3 target roles, audit LinkedIn.

Week 2 — Evidence

Ubah 5–8 bullets menjadi CAR dan siapkan 6 STAR stories.

Week 3 — Network

Pilih 20 relevant professionals/alumni dan mulai 5 genuine conversations.

Week 4 — Pipeline

Set alerts, targeted applications, track conversion, mock interview.
